Liberty settings bridge. This page is opened invisibly by the Liberty HUD mod inside Deadlock's built-in browser. It keeps the mod's settings in this browser's own storage on the player's computer.

It also keeps two anonymous counts (GoatCounter): that a game launch happened (at most once every 6 hours per player), and — only after a ranked match — the player's broad rank tier, a number from 1 to 11 with no sub-rank (at most once every 20 hours). No nickname, Steam ID, match id or settings are ever sent. Both counts can be turned off in the Liberty menu, under About. It sends nothing else anywhere.
